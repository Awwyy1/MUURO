You can add them gradually. The gallery shows whatever is here, in
order. The site reads this folder automatically at build time. No
code changes needed. See `public/editions/README.md` for the full
how-to and format guidance.

## Shot list for this edition

The piece is two overprinted marks: DO in ink, IT in the counters.
Photography should make that readable at thumbnail size and again up
close.

- `01.jpg` **cover.** Framed, straight on, matte black frame, plain
  wall. Full sheet in frame, nothing cropped. This is the one that
  sells the concept in the grid, so the counters have to read clean.
- `02.jpg` **on a wall, at distance.** Far enough that the eye reads
  DO first. Desk, chair or a doorway edge in frame for scale.
- `03.jpg` **angled detail of one glyph.** Close on the D and its
  counter, raking light, so the paper texture and the ink edge show.
- `04.jpg` **frame corner.** Mitred Nielsen corner, glazing edge,
  print margin. Proof of build quality.
- `05.jpg` **in a working room.** Above a desk, over a monitor, next
  to a shelf. A room someone actually works in, not a showroom.
- `06.jpg` **held in hands.** Print only version, no frame, someone
  holding the sheet. Gives scale and makes the tubed option real.
- `07.jpg` to `10.jpg` optional. Backlit at night (A2 or A1 only),
  print rolled next to its tube, second frame finish in brushed
  silver, or a wide interior with the piece small in the corner.

Portrait 4:5 crops, at least 1600 px on the long side, under 600 KB
each.
