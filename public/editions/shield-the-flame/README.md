## The message on the sheet

Plain text, 40 words, 168 characters, 435 Morse symbols (258 dots,
177 dashes):

> Love is not a fire that burns everything down, it is a candle that
> teaches two souls how to survive the dark. The trick is not finding
> someone who lights the flame, but someone who protects it from the
> wind.

International Morse, letters separated by a space, words by a slash,
comma is `--..--` and full stop is `.-.-.-`:

The line breaks above are for this file only. On the print the code
runs continuously and wraps wherever the grid wraps.

Proportions for the print: a dash is three dots long, the space
inside a letter is one dot, between letters three, between words
seven. Keep them honest and the sheet decodes; fudge them and it
becomes decoration.

The listing on the site does not carry the translation. That is
deliberate, so do not paste it into the product copy.

## Shot list for this edition

The piece is a field of code. Photography has to sell texture from far
away and legibility up close.

- `01.jpg` **cover.** Framed, straight on, plain wall. The whole grid
  in frame. At thumbnail size it should read as a woven pattern.
- `02.jpg` **macro of a few rows.** Close enough to count dots. This
  is the shot that tells a buyer the code is real and not decoration.
- `03.jpg` **in a bedroom or hallway.** Warm lamp, evening. It is a
  gift piece, so it needs one photo that feels like a home rather than
  a product.
- `04.jpg` **backlit at night** (A2 or A1). Room lights off. Dots and
  dashes glowing is the strongest argument for the light option.
- `05.jpg` **frame corner.** Mitred Nielsen corner, glazing edge, ink
  on paper at the margin.
- `06.jpg` **scale shot.** Above a bed or a sofa, whole wall in frame.
- `07.jpg` to `10.jpg` optional. The amber mark in macro, the print
  being handed over, the sheet unrolled from its tube, or the piece
  paired with another edition on the same wall.

Portrait 4:5 crops, at least 1600 px on the long side, under 600 KB
each.
